# AI Ready Quiz Portal — Student Guide
**Institution:** T.S.D.H. (Technical Skill Development Hub), SVIT Nashik  
**Portal Access:** Works on any Smartphone (Chrome, Safari, Edge) or Laptop

---

## 1. Getting Started: Registration & Login

1. Open the portal URL shared by your workshop coordinator.
2. If it's your first time, click **Student Register**:
   - Enter your **Full Name** (as on college ID).
   - Enter your **Roll Number / PRN** (e.g., `SVIT2024CS001`).
   - Enter your **College Email** and select your **Department** & **Year** (FE, SE, TE, BE).
   - Set a password (min 6 characters).
   - Enter the **Workshop Access Code**: **`TSDH2026`** (announced on the workshop screen).
3. Click **Complete Registration & Enter**.
4. If already registered, simply use **Student Login** with your Roll Number and Password.

---

## 2. Before Starting a Quiz (Checklist)

1. **Battery:** Ensure your phone or laptop has at least 30% battery.
2. **Camera Access:** When prompted, click **Allow** for the front camera. The portal captures periodic verification snapshots (no video is recorded or streamed publicly).
3. **Close Background Apps:** Close WhatsApp, Instagram, browsers tabs, or split-screen windows to avoid accidental tab-switch violations.
4. **Fullscreen Consent:** Click **Enter Fullscreen & Begin Exam**.

---

## 3. During the Quiz

- **Timer:** Located in the top right corner. Turns amber when < 5 mins left, and pulses red when < 2 mins left.
- **Auto-Submit:** If your timer hits `00:00`, all your answered questions are automatically submitted.
- **Auto-Save:** Every answer you tap is saved instantly. Even if your internet connection drops, you can continue answering offline! When internet resumes, answers sync automatically.
- **Review Later:** Tap **Review Later** on tricky questions. The question number turns amber on your Question Palette so you can return to it easily.
- **Question Palette:** Tap the **Palette** button to see all question numbers:
  - 🟢 **Green:** Answered
  - 🟡 **Yellow:** Marked for Review
  - ⚪ **Dark:** Unanswered
  - 🟣 **Purple Ring:** Current Question

---

## 4. Proctoring Rules: Avoid Disqualification!

The exam environment is strictly audited:
- ❌ **Do NOT exit fullscreen:** Exiting fullscreen mode triggers a warning.
- ❌ **Do NOT switch tabs or apps:** Checking messages or switching tabs logs an immediate infraction.
- ❌ **Do NOT attempt to copy/paste:** Keyboard shortcuts and right-click are disabled.
- ⚠️ **3 Violations Limit:** If you accumulate 3 violations, the portal will automatically submit and lock your exam!

---

## 5. After the Quiz: Results & Certificate

1. **Instant Score:** View your score, percentage, passing status, and time taken.
2. **Detailed Explanations:** Scroll down to review each question with the correct answer and a concept explanation.
3. **Official PDF Certificate:** If you score 60% or higher, click **Download Official Certificate (PDF)** to save your verifiable certificate with TSDH & SVIT Nashik seals.
4. **WhatsApp Sharing:** Click **Share on WhatsApp** to celebrate your score with classmates!
5. **Leaderboard:** Check where you stand among all students in the workshop cohort!
